Frequently asked

Do I need an account to use Showcase?
No. There's no sign-up, no login, and nothing tied to an email address. Open the app and start cataloguing.

How do I add a watch?
Tap the crown button in the top right of The Collection. You can photograph the piece against the built-in dial guide, or skip the photo and fill in the details later.

What's free and what's Pro?
The free case holds 8 pieces with full cataloguing. Showcase Pro is a one-time purchase that unlocks unlimited pieces plus five tools: Wear Rotation, Service Due, Strap Log, Grail List, and a full collection export.

How do I restore my Pro purchase on a new device?
Open any locked Pro tool and tap Restore Purchase.

How does iCloud sync work?
It's optional. When it's on, your collection copies to your own private iCloud, so it shows up on every device signed into your Apple ID. Nobody but you can see it.

What happens to my photos and data if I delete the app?
Anything stored only on-device is removed with the app, as with any iOS app. If iCloud sync was on, your collection remains in your private iCloud until you clear it from there as well.

How do service reminders work?
Set a last-serviced date and a service interval on any piece, and Showcase schedules a local reminder on your device — no server involved, so it works even offline.
